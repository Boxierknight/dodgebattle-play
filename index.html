YOUR TURN

  • Click a red player holding a ball, then a blue player, then THROW!
  • Or click a teammate to hand off the ball to a better arm.
  • END TURN, then choose whether to move players into open spots. CONFIRM hands it to Blue.

THE DICE

  • Every throw is d20 + modifier against the defender's d20 + modifier. Ties go to the defender.
  • Rows matter: Front throws +1 but defends −1. Back throws −1 but defends +1.
  • Hover a target to see your exact odds before you throw.

DEFENDING

  • A red ring means the CPU is throwing at that player. Pick Dodge, Catch, or Block (with a ball).
  • Catch it and the thrower is out, and your first player out comes back in.
  • Knock out all six to win.
